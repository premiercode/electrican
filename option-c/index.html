Sheet E-301 · Details

EV chargers and standby generators, drawn out

The two projects people plan the most. Here is what goes into each one.

Detail 2

Level 2 EV charger circuit

  1. Level 2 runs on 240 volts, the same as a dryer or oven.
  2. It needs its own dedicated circuit, sized at 125% of the charger's rating. A 40-amp charger needs a 50-amp circuit.
  3. Plug-in (NEMA 14-50 outlet) or hardwired. Chargers of 48 amps and up are usually hardwired.
  4. A load calculation checks the panel first. 100-amp service is often enough, depending on your other appliances.
  5. Outdoor installation is safe with outdoor-rated equipment.

Detail 3

Generator through a transfer switch

  1. A generator must connect through a transfer switch, never a wall outlet. Without one, power can backfeed into utility lines and seriously injure or kill line workers.
  2. With an automatic transfer switch, the generator must be sized to carry everything the switch transfers, unless the loads are managed.
