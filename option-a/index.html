CKT 03 Quick guide

Is it an emergency?

Read from the top. Stop at the first line that matches what you see.

  1. 1

    Call 911 now and get out

    • Smoke, flames or a sounding smoke alarm: get out, stay out, call 911.
    • A downed or low power line: stay away, keep others and pets away, call 911, then your utility.
  2. 2

    Stop using it and call a licensed electrician now

    • Sparks from an outlet.
    • A burning or rubbery smell from an outlet, switch or appliance. Call 911 if there is smoke or heat.
    • Warm, discolored, cracked or broken outlets or switches.
    • A tingle or shock when you touch an appliance, sink or fixture.
    Call (469) 555-0132 24/7 line (Sample)
  3. 3

    Book an electrician soon

    • Flickering or dimming lights.
    • A breaker that trips again and again. Find out why before resetting it again.

  4. 4

    Power out?

    • Check your breakers first, then report it to your electric utility.
    • In Frisco that is Oncor (888-313-4747, or text OUT to 66267) or CoServ (855-938-3496), depending on your address.

CKT 04 EV chargers & generators

Charge in the garage. Keep the lights on.

Level 2 EV charger

25–40

miles of range, roughly, per hour of charging on Level 2.

40A charger × 125% 50A circuit
  • 240 volts, the same as a dryer or oven, on its own dedicated circuit.
  • Plug-in (NEMA 14-50 outlet) or hardwired. Chargers of 48 amps and up are usually hardwired.
  • Outdoor installation is safe with outdoor-rated equipment.
  • A load calculation checks your panel first. 100-amp service is often enough, depending on your other appliances.

Standby generator

A generator must connect through a transfer switch. Never through a wall outlet.

  • Without a transfer switch, power can backfeed into utility lines and seriously injure or kill line workers.
  • With an automatic transfer switch, the generator must be sized to carry everything the switch transfers, unless the loads are managed.
